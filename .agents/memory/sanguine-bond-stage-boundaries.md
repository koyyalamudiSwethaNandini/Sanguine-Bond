---
name: Sanguine Bond Stage 1 boundaries
description: Product constraints for the Sanguine Bond emergency blood coordination prototype.
---

Sanguine Bond is a coordination and donor-prioritization interface only. Stage 1 uses fictional local demo data and UI-only interactions; do not add backend storage, real authentication, APIs, real AI, external maps, real notifications, or payments without a later explicit stage request.

The product must never determine or imply medical donor eligibility, blood compatibility, cross-match results, transfusion safety, or whether someone can donate. Qualified healthcare and blood-bank professionals retain all screening, testing, compatibility verification, and transfusion decisions. Donor availability is self-reported; exact donor home addresses must never appear.

**Why:** The product brief explicitly limits Stage 1 to a UI prototype and sets healthcare-safety and donor-location privacy boundaries.

**How to apply:** Preserve these constraints in future feature work; any later real coordination or clinical workflows need explicit scope and professional oversight.
