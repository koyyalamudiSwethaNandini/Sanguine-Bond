Build Stage 1 of a professional web application called:

TAGLINE:
"Connecting Blood. Connecting Lives."

IMPORTANT:
This is Stage 1 only.
Do NOT implement backend, database, authentication, APIs, real AI APIs, payment systems, real-time notifications, or external map APIs yet.

The goal of Stage 1 is to create a complete, polished, responsive UI/UX prototype with realistic sample data and working page navigation.

PROJECT CONTEXT:

Sanguine Bond is an emergency blood coordination platform designed to help hospitals, blood banks, patients/authorized attendants, and registered blood donors coordinate during urgent blood requirements.

The platform should help users identify potentially suitable registered donors based on:
- Blood group
- Required blood component
- Donor availability
- Approximate location/distance
- Emergency urgency

IMPORTANT MEDICAL SAFETY BOUNDARY:

Sanguine Bond is only a coordination and donor-prioritization platform.

It must NOT claim to determine:
- Medical donor eligibility
- Final blood compatibility
- Cross-matching results
- Transfusion safety
- Whether a person can donate blood

All medical screening, blood testing, cross-matching, compatibility verification, and final transfusion decisions must remain with qualified healthcare and blood-bank professionals.
